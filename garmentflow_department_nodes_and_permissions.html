💡 What Does a "Node" Mean in This Factory Setup?

In manufacturing ERP engineering, a "Node" is 1 physical computer, workstation, or terminal placed in a specific department. When 12 nodes are planned, it means the factory will install 12 dedicated computers across the plant. Each computer is locked to its department's duties so workers only see the tools they need to perform their daily tasks, while company financials and profit margins remain strictly confidential.

★ Architecture Decision 1
👑 3 Admin Nodes — 100% Identical Full Super Admin Rights
Nodes 10, 11 & 12 (MD Cabin, Partner Cabin, GM Cabin) operate with identical, unrestricted Super Admin authority across both the Cloud Owner Portal and Floor Factory Stations.
  • ✓ Zero Operational Friction: All 3 key leaders can approve high-value POs, sign off vendor releases, and inspect financials without waiting for an absent colleague's credentials.
  • ✓ Consolidated Visibility: Instant access to real-time factory P&L, balance sheets, order margins, delivery health, and floor stoppage alerts.
  • ✓ Complete Security Governance: Full authority to add/edit user accounts, assign workstation roles, and review tamper-proof audit trails.
★ Architecture Decision 2
💼👥 Accounts & HR — Unified into ONE Combined Department
Nodes 08 & 09 are not artificially split into separate islands. Instead, they form a single integrated administrative section with identical shared permissions across both terminals.
  • ✓ Integrated Garment Economics: Garment manufacturing links raw fabric bills and buyer GST invoices directly with contractor wage bills and operator piece-work muster rolls.
  • ✓ Mutual Cross-Cover: During month-end payroll or urgent dispatch billing surges, both terminal operators can process GST tax invoices or salary slips simultaneously.
  • ✓ Zero Inter-Department Silos: Live synchronization between attendance biometric logs, overtime calculation, worker payouts, and company ledger debits.

Core Security & Floor Segregation Principles
How GarmentFlow safeguards factory operations and financial data across the 12 nodes:

🔒 Commercial Privacy

Factory floor workers on Nodes 03 to 07 cannot view buyer order values, fabric purchasing costs, profit margins, or company bank balances.

⚡ One-Click Workstation Landing

When a workstation boots up on the floor (e.g., Cutting Node 04), it lands directly on its dedicated module with clean zero-confusion navigation.

🛡️ Strict Segregation of Duties

Store cannot alter buyer invoices; Accounts cannot change cutting marker parameters; Merchandising cannot approve payroll muster rolls.
